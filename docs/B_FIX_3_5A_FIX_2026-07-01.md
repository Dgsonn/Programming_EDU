Verify sau full pipeline:
- `WHERE dlc_no=2 AND ref_game_id=300` → 1 row (Blood and Wine) ✓
- `WHERE dlc_no=2` (1 cond, backward) → 7 rows ✓ (A8 hint khớp data mới)
